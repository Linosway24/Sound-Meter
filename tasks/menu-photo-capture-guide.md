## 💡 Tips for Good Photos

- **Make sure LCD is clear** - good lighting so screen is readable
- **Show full screen** - including soft key labels at bottom
- **Avoid glare** - angle camera to minimize reflections
- **Focus on LCD** - make sure text is sharp and readable
- **Multiple angles if needed** - if soft keys aren't visible, take another shot
